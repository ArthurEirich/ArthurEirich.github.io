Computer help for seniors in Zurich

Patient, personal help at home with computers, phones, email and WhatsApp in Zurich. Older customers and family members can arrange a visit.

CHF 130 / hour for private customers The standard private rate is CHF 32.50 per 15 minutes, including computer help at home.

Request help +41 76 841 44 68 WhatsApp

What I can help with

Use your devices at your own pace

I help set up computers, smartphones and printers at home. We can practise the things you want to do, such as sending email, calling family on WhatsApp, making a video call, viewing photos or finding information online.

Make the screen easier to use and stay safe online

We can adjust text size, contrast and other accessibility options. I can also show you how to manage passwords, spot suspicious messages and check whether an email or phone call is genuine.

Arrange help for a family member

A relative can make the enquiry and join the visit if wanted. We agree what the person receiving help wants to learn or change. A follow-up can be arranged if more questions come up later.

How it works

Tell me two or three things you’d like to be able to do. I’ll explain the steps and give you time to try them on your own device. If helpful, we’ll recap the most important settings and next steps before I leave.

I offer on-site help in Zurich and roughly 20 km around Oerlikon. We’ll discuss whether remote support is suitable. Support in English and German.

Prices and billing

Remote support has a 15-minute minimum and no travel fee. On-site visits have a 60-minute minimum and one call-out fee per appointment. Additional time is billed in 15-minute increments. See rates and billing terms.

The 15-minute introductory consultation by phone or WhatsApp is free and non-binding. Technical troubleshooting and remote support are paid services.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous computer experience?

No. The session starts with what you want to do and the experience you already have.

Can I book for my parents or grandparents?

Yes. A family member can arrange the visit with the agreement of the person receiving help.

Is there a separate rate for seniors?

The usual private customer rate applies, including the on-site minimum and call-out fee.
