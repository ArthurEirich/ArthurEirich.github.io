What I can help with

Set up a Mac and move your files

I can help set up a MacBook, iMac, Mac mini or Mac Studio, configure macOS and updates, and migrate from another Mac or a Windows computer. If the Mac is slow or running out of space, we’ll check storage, software and settings.

Understand your Apple Account and iCloud

I help with Apple Account settings, iCloud Drive and iCloud Photos, and explain what syncs across Mac, iPhone and iPad. If you’ve lost account access, I can guide you through Apple’s official recovery procedures.

Make your Apple devices work together

We can set up AirDrop, Handoff and other Continuity features, plus AirPlay and Apple TV where appropriate. I can also help with Family Sharing, Screen Time, parental controls, Wi-Fi, printers and peripherals.

Back up with Time Machine

I can configure Time Machine and help restore files from an existing backup. Before a migration, we can check which files are on the computer, in your backups or synced to iCloud.

How it works

In the introductory consultation we’ll establish which devices and accounts are involved and what you want to work day to day. During a migration, keep the old device available until you’ve checked your important files on the new Mac.

I offer on-site help in Zurich and roughly 20 km around Oerlikon. We’ll discuss whether remote support is suitable. Support in English and German.

The 15-minute introductory consultation by phone or WhatsApp is free and non-binding. Technical troubleshooting and remote support are paid services.

Frequently asked questions

Are you an authorised Apple service provider?

Eirich IT is an independent IT service. I help with setup and software and don’t offer physical MacBook or iPhone repairs.

Can you recover access to my Apple Account?

I can guide you through Apple’s official process. Recovery depends on the account and available information. I don’t bypass Activation Lock.

Does iCloud replace a backup?

iCloud can sync data across devices. We’ll look at which files can also be backed up and what you can restore from a backup.
