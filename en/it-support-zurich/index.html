Help with devices, software and data

Need to set up a user account on a new laptop, fix an app that stopped working after an update, or move your data to a new phone? I help individuals and small businesses set up devices and troubleshoot software problems.

  • PCs and laptops – Set up devices, install software and diagnose software problems.
  • Smartphones and tablets – Set up profiles and apps, transfer data and create a backup.
  • Backups and data protection – Set up a backup that fits how you use your devices, then run a short restore test to check that you can recover your data.

On-site support in Zurich or remote help

Based in Zurich Oerlikon, I can visit your home or office by arrangement. I cover Zurich and the surrounding area within about 20 km, including Wallisellen, Opfikon and Dübendorf.

If the device is online and accessible remotely, I can investigate many software problems without a visit. In our first call, we’ll go over what you’re seeing and decide which approach makes sense.

Frequently asked questions

Do you repair broken hardware?

I help with setup and software problems. Hardware repairs such as replacing a screen are outside the scope of this service.

What should I have ready for the first call?

The device or model, operating system and exact wording of any error message are helpful. If the problem started after a change, tell me what changed.

Do you offer IT support for small businesses?

Yes. I work with individuals and small businesses. The price list includes hourly rates and call-out fees.
