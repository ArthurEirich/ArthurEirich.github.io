What I can help with

Set up a Windows PC or laptop

I can install and configure Windows, set up user profiles and email, install software and help move your files. We’ll check which settings, apps and licences you need on the new computer.

Find out why Windows is slow or unreliable

I investigate failed updates, driver issues, software errors and slow starts. Depending on the symptoms, I check storage, startup apps and the computer’s security settings.

Choose remote support or an on-site visit

With a working internet connection, we can check many settings remotely. If the PC won’t start or your connection is part of the problem, we can discuss an on-site appointment.

How it works

Tell me which device and Windows version you use, what changed and whether there’s an error message. We’ll agree how to proceed and what it will cost. After a migration we can check that your important files and accounts are available.

I offer on-site help in Zurich and roughly 20 km around Oerlikon. We’ll discuss whether remote support is suitable. Support in English and German.

Prices and billing

Remote support has a 15-minute minimum and no travel fee. On-site visits have a 60-minute minimum and one call-out fee per appointment. Additional time is billed in 15-minute increments. See rates and billing terms.

The 15-minute introductory consultation by phone or WhatsApp is free and non-binding. Technical troubleshooting and remote support are paid services.

Frequently asked questions

Can you reinstall Windows?

Yes, by arrangement. We first discuss backups, the software you need and whether your licence suits the installation.

Can you replace a broken screen or drive?

My work covers setup and software troubleshooting. Physical repairs are outside the scope of this service.

What do I need for remote support?

A usable PC and a stable internet connection. We’ll use the initial consultation to check whether a remote session makes sense.
