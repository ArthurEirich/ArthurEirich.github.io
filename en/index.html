Frequently asked questions

What does PC help in Zurich cost?

Private clients pay CHF 100 per hour, business clients CHF 140 per hour, billed in 15-minute steps. Typical jobs have fixed prices, e.g. phone setup from CHF 129 or Wi-Fi/router from CHF 189. The first phone call (15 minutes) is free.

Do you come to my home or office?

Yes, in Zurich and the surrounding area (approx. 20 km from Oerlikon, e.g. Dübendorf, Wallisellen, Opfikon, Kloten, Regensdorf, Schlieren, Dietikon, Uster). A call-out fee of CHF 20 to 50 applies depending on distance. Many problems can also be solved remotely.

How quickly can I get help?

I usually reply the same day. I'm available daily from 10:00 to 20:00 by phone, WhatsApp, email or the contact form.

What problems do you solve?

Software-level setup and repair for phones, tablets, PCs and laptops, Wi-Fi/router/network, plus backup and data protection. Hardware repairs (e.g. screen replacement) are not included.

Do you also build websites and software?

Yes. Websites and landing pages from CHF 1,490, custom software and automation from CHF 2,500 – after a short, free initial call you get a quote.

Is VAT charged?

No. All prices are final prices, no VAT (MWST) is charged (turnover below the VAT liability threshold of CHF 100,000).
