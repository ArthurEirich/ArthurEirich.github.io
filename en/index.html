Frequently asked questions

What does IT support in Zurich cost?

Private customers pay CHF 130 per hour (CHF 32.50 per 15 minutes). Business customers pay CHF 160 per hour (CHF 40 per 15 minutes). Remote support has a 15-minute minimum and no travel fee. On-site visits have a 60-minute minimum and one call-out fee per appointment. Additional time is billed in 15-minute increments.

What’s included in the free introductory consultation?

We spend 15 minutes discussing the issue and next steps by phone or WhatsApp, with no obligation. Technical troubleshooting and remote support are paid services.

Do you visit homes and offices?

Yes, in Zurich and the surrounding area, roughly 20 km from Oerlikon. Examples include Dübendorf, Wallisellen, Opfikon, Kloten, Regensdorf, Schlieren, Dietikon and Uster. The call-out fee depends on distance.

When can I reach you?

You can reach me daily from 10:00 to 20:00 by phone, WhatsApp, email or the contact form.

Which devices do you support?

I help with Windows, macOS, iOS/iPadOS and Android, as well as Wi-Fi, backups and printers. I cover setup and software issues. Physical repairs are outside the service.

Do you build websites and software?

Yes. Websites and landing pages start at CHF 1'490. Custom software and automation start at CHF 2'500. The individual quote sets out the scope and price.

Is VAT charged?

No. All prices are final prices. No VAT (MWST) is charged (turnover below the VAT liability threshold of CHF 100,000).

Consent and website statistics

Analytics is optional. I use it with your permission to understand site use, performance and how people find or contact me. Your choice does not affect access to the site or contact options. On this site, Privacy settings remembers only your choice, the policy version and the decision date in browser storage. No tracking cookie or visitor identifier is set by the consent controls. If storage is unavailable, analytics stays off.

Analytics starts on the next page load after you allow it. To withdraw consent, open Privacy settings in the footer and select “Necessary only”. If the Cloudflare beacon was loaded, the page reloads to stop it. A request already sent or queued during unloading may still arrive, and withdrawal does not erase earlier statistics. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track override a stored permission.

When configured, Cloudflare Web Analytics uses its browser beacon to measure page views, traffic sources and loading performance. Cloudflare documents seven days of unsampled beacon data and statistics available for up to six months. Cloudflare Web Analytics documentation

Custom conversion tracking is currently disabled.

Custom conversion tracking runs only after you allow analytics.

When enabled, custom conversion tracking counts contact-link clicks, confirmed or failed form submissions and landings with recognized campaign tags. Cloudflare Analytics Engine retains the event type, canonical page, language, allowed source or referral category, allowed medium and campaign, preset service and server-generated time for three months. The service comes from the service page or the preset topic selected at submission. Attribution is kept in memory only for the current page. The dataset excludes IP addresses, browser identifiers, contact details, message contents, full URLs and visitor identifiers. Analytics Engine retention

Cloudflare delivers and protects the site and provides its analytics infrastructure. Its systems can process IP addresses, browser information, requested URLs and other technical data for delivery, security and operations. Cloudflare's edge rate limit uses IP addresses to protect the custom analytics endpoint. This infrastructure processing is separate from the conversion dataset. Operational retention depends on the applicable service and settings. Cloudflare privacy policy · Cloudflare data processing addendum
