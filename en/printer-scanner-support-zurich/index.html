What I can help with

Install a USB, Wi-Fi or wired network printer

I help set up printers on Windows and macOS, checking drivers, print queues and the connection according to the model. I can connect a printer to an existing home or small-office network.

Print from a phone or tablet

Where the printer supports it, I can set up mobile printing, including AirPrint for iPhone and iPad. We’ll test printing from the device you actually use.

Set up scanning or troubleshoot an offline printer

I can configure a scanner and set up scan-to-computer where supported. For an offline printer, I check whether the cause is the connection, driver, print queue or the printer itself.

How it works

Tell me the printer or scanner model, operating system and how the device is connected. After setup, we’ll try a print and, if needed, send a scan to the computer you use.

I offer on-site help in Zurich and roughly 20 km around Oerlikon. We’ll discuss whether remote support is suitable. Support in English and German.

Prices and billing

Remote support has a 15-minute minimum and no travel fee. On-site visits have a 60-minute minimum and one call-out fee per appointment. Additional time is billed in 15-minute increments. See rates and billing terms.

The 15-minute introductory consultation by phone or WhatsApp is free and non-binding. Technical troubleshooting and remote support are paid services.

Frequently asked questions

Will an older printer work with my computer?

That depends on the model, available drivers and the version of Windows or macOS. I’ll check what options are available.

Can I print from iPhone or Android?

Yes, if your printer and device support it. Which method works depends on the printer, mobile device and network.

Do you fix paper jams or replace broken parts?

I cover setup, software and connectivity. Mechanical printer repairs are outside my service.
