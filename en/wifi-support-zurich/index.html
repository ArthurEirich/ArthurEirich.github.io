What I can help with

Find the cause of slow or unreliable Wi-Fi

Do calls only drop in the home office, or is the internet slow throughout the property? I check whether the problem is with a device, Wi-Fi or the broadband connection. We can compare reception across rooms or test a connection directly at the router.

Set up routers, FRITZ!Box and mesh systems

I can help configure your router, FRITZ!Box, mesh nodes or repeaters. We’ll review placement and settings, and set up a guest network if you need one.

Connect devices to an existing network

I help bring printers, phones, smart devices and office computers onto an existing home or small-office network. Checking more than one device helps narrow down the problem.

How it works

Tell me your router model and which devices or rooms are affected. An on-site visit helps when reception varies by room. Results depend on the building, internet connection and devices, so I don’t promise a specific speed or coverage.

I offer on-site help in Zurich and roughly 20 km around Oerlikon. We’ll discuss whether remote support is suitable. Support in English and German.

Prices and billing

Remote support has a 15-minute minimum and no travel fee. On-site visits have a 60-minute minimum and one call-out fee per appointment. Additional time is billed in 15-minute increments. See rates and billing terms.

The 15-minute introductory consultation by phone or WhatsApp is free and non-binding. Technical troubleshooting and remote support are paid services.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a new router or mesh system?

We’ll diagnose the cause first. Placement or settings may help. Mesh is one option for rooms that the existing Wi-Fi doesn’t reach well.

Can you help if the broadband connection is completely down?

I can check the router and devices on site. Your internet provider handles faults with the broadband connection itself.

Can you set up my FRITZ!Box?

Yes. We’ll check the model, the type of connection and any access details you already have, then discuss which settings or devices are needed.
