When Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping

If video calls drop or Wi-Fi is weak in certain rooms, the cause could be the wireless signal, a particular device or the internet connection. We’ll look at which devices are affected and where or when the problem happens.

  • Router setup – Get help setting up a new router and connecting your devices.
  • Wi-Fi coverage – Check whether the router’s placement and settings suit the rooms where you use Wi-Fi.
  • Network troubleshooting – Narrow down whether the problem affects one device, the wireless network or the internet connection.

Wi-Fi help at home or in a small office

An on-site visit is often useful for coverage problems because I can check reception where you work or live. I visit customers in Zurich and around Oerlikon, including Wallisellen, Opfikon and Kloten.

For the first call, it helps to know your router model and which devices and rooms are affected. If the connection is stable enough, we can discuss which settings can be checked remotely.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to buy a new router?

That depends on the cause. We’ll first check which devices are affected and whether the router’s position or settings could explain the problem.

Can you help if the internet is completely down?

I can troubleshoot the router and devices on site. If the fault is with the internet connection itself, your internet provider handles the repair.
