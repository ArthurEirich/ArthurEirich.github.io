What I can help with

Set up an iPhone, iPad or Android device

I help set up accounts, install the apps you need and configure email, contacts and calendars. I can also connect your device to Wi-Fi and a compatible printer.

Bring photos, contacts and WhatsApp across

Before switching phones, we check which photos, contacts and other data are backed up. I help with moves between iOS and Android, and with WhatsApp setup and chat transfer where its official tools support your devices and accounts.

Adjust security and accessibility

I’ll explain privacy and security settings in plain language. We can also set up larger text, display options, parental controls or other accessibility features.

How it works

Have both devices, a charger and access to the relevant accounts ready. Keep the old device as it is until we’ve checked the important data. Please leave passwords and verification codes out of your enquiry.

I offer on-site help in Zurich and roughly 20 km around Oerlikon. We’ll discuss whether remote support is suitable. Support in English and German.

Prices and billing

Remote support has a 15-minute minimum and no travel fee. On-site visits have a 60-minute minimum and one call-out fee per appointment. Additional time is billed in 15-minute increments. See rates and billing terms.

The 15-minute introductory consultation by phone or WhatsApp is free and non-binding. Technical troubleshooting and remote support are paid services.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help me switch between iPhone and Android?

Yes. We’ll check in advance what can move based on the devices, accounts and backups you have.

Will my WhatsApp chats definitely transfer?

It depends on the supported transfer method, accounts and backups. I can guide you through the official steps and check with you what’s available afterwards.

Can you replace a screen or bypass a device lock?

I offer setup, migration and help with settings. Physical repairs and bypassing device security are outside the service.
