What I can help with

A website that answers your customers’ questions

I work with small businesses and independent professionals, including consultants and local beauty or wellness providers. We decide what customers need to know, which services warrant separate pages and how enquiries should reach you.

What the starter one-page website includes

The starting price covers a mobile-friendly one-page website, a contact form and a basic structure for your legal notice and privacy policy. It does not include legal advice or a review of your legal text.

Plan technical SEO, launch and ongoing changes

During scoping we discuss performance, page titles and structure, hosting and domain configuration, and deployment. Multi-page business sites, for example three to five pages, Google Maps, additional SEO work, ongoing maintenance and future improvements are quoted separately as needed.

How it works

Share any existing copy, photos or website. We agree the scope and how visitors should contact you, review the site at different screen sizes and arrange how it will go live.

I’m based in Zurich Oerlikon and work with businesses in the city and nearby. We can plan the project remotely. Support in English and German.

Prices and billing

The starting price applies to the one-page scope described above. See rates and billing terms.

The 15-minute introductory consultation by phone or WhatsApp is free and non-binding. Technical troubleshooting and remote support are paid services.

Frequently asked questions

Is hosting included in the starting price?

The starter scope covers a mobile-friendly one-page site, a form and a basic legal-page structure. We specify hosting, domain and configuration in the quote.

Can my services have separate pages?

Yes. We can discuss a multi-page site, the copy each page needs and the technical SEO scope before arranging a quote.

Can the site be extended later?

Yes. Maintenance and future changes can be agreed separately.
